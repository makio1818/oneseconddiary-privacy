Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app “1秒日記” (OneSecondDiary, the “App”) handles your information. The App is provided by the developer of OneSecondDiary (the “Developer”, “we”).

At a glance

  • No sign-up or login is required.
  • The videos you record are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to our servers.
  • The App contains no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

01App name and contact

  • App name: 1秒日記 / OneSecondDiary
  • Provided by: the developer of OneSecondDiary
  • Contact: Kanri.connect18@gmail.com

02No account required

The App does not require you to create an account or log in. It never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

03Where your videos and records are stored

The App stores the following information in its own storage area on your iPhone:

  • The one-second clips you record (cut from videos you choose and converted) and their thumbnail images
  • Record details such as the day, the original video’s capture date, the original video’s identifier in your photo library, the start position of the clip, and whether it has sound
  • App settings such as reminder on/off and time, and date display
  • Whether you have purchased the Pro upgrade

None of this information is sent to our servers, and we cannot access or view it.

04Why the App accesses your photo library

The App accesses your photo library only for these purposes:

  • To show the videos taken on the day you want to record, so you can choose one (read access)
  • To save the movies you make to the Photos app when you ask it to (add access)

You can also record without granting photo library access by choosing videos one at a time in the photo picker. If you choose “Limited Access”, only the photos you allowed are shown. The App never changes or deletes the original photos and videos in your library.

05How videos are processed and saved

  • The App cuts about one second (0.5–1 second) from the video you choose and converts it to a portrait 1080×1920, 30 fps format. Landscape and square videos are fitted with margins. HDR videos are converted to standard color (SDR). If the original video has sound, the sound is kept.
  • Cutting, converting and making movies all happen on your iPhone. Your videos are never sent to an external server.
  • Movies are made by joining your saved one-second clips in date order. Movies made with the free version show the app name at the end.
  • Only when you choose to, you can save a movie to the Photos app or send it to apps or people you pick using the standard iOS share sheet. Shared content is then handled under the terms and policies of the service you share to.
  • Temporary files created during processing are deleted afterwards.

06Videos from iCloud Photos

If you use iCloud Photos and the full video you choose is not stored on your iPhone, the App downloads it from iCloud through the iOS Photos framework (PhotoKit). This connection is between iOS and Apple and does not go through our servers. The downloaded video is then processed and saved on your iPhone as described above.

07Notifications

Notifications are used only as a daily reminder to record your second. They are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone and are not sent on days you have already recorded. No push notification server is used. You can turn notifications off at any time in the App’s settings or in the iOS Settings app.

08In-app purchase (StoreKit 2) and Apple payment processing

  • The App offers a one-time in-app purchase (Pro) that unlocks unlimited days and more, using Apple’s StoreKit 2. There are no subscriptions.
  • All payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details, such as credit card information, or your Apple Account information.
  • Loading product information, purchasing, checking your purchase and restoring purchases are handled between StoreKit and Apple. The result (whether you have purchased) is stored on your iPhone.
  • Purchase information is handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

09iCloud Backup

Your recorded videos, record details and settings are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer). If iCloud Backup is on, this data is stored in Apple’s iCloud as part of the backup. Backups are provided and managed by Apple, and we cannot see their contents. The App has no iCloud sync feature of its own.

10Advertising and analytics SDKs

  • The App uses no advertising SDKs and shows no ads.
  • The App uses no analytics SDKs, such as usage analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • The App is built only with standard iOS frameworks, without third-party libraries (SDKs).
  • The App does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

11Data sent to external servers

The App does not send your recorded videos or any information about you to our servers or to any third-party servers. The App’s only network connections are the following connections to Apple, made through standard iOS features:

  • Downloading videos from iCloud Photos
  • Loading product information, purchasing, and checking or restoring purchases on the App Store

When you choose to share a movie or send an email yourself, the information goes to the recipient you choose.

12When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address, your name (if provided) and the content of your message only to reply to you and to investigate problems. We do not share it with third parties except where required by law.

When you open the email form from the App, the App version, iOS version and device model are filled into the message in advance. You can review or delete them before sending, and nothing reaches us unless you send the email.

13When you delete the App

Deleting the App deletes all records (videos, thumbnails and record details) and settings stored on your iPhone. Because we keep no data on any server, we cannot restore them. You can also delete individual records by choosing a day in the Calendar.

  • Movies you saved to the Photos app remain in the Photos app.
  • iPhone backups made before deletion contain the data as of that time.
  • The Pro purchase is tied to your Apple Account and can be restored with “Restore Purchase” after reinstalling.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law or in the App’s features. If there are important changes, we will let you know in the App or on this page. The updated policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
